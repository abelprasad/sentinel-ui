How Sentinel works

1
ADS-B ingestion
Live aircraft telemetry (position, altitude, speed, heading) streamed from public ADS-B receivers.
→
2
Behavioral baselines
Per-aircraft profiles learn normal altitude, speed, and heading envelopes over time.
→
3
Anomaly scoring
Each new position is scored against its baseline. Deviations surface as scored incidents.
→
4
AI explanation
An LLM turns raw deviations into a plain-English assessment of what went off-profile.
→
5
Live dashboard
Tracks, scores, and explanations stream here. Replay any incident on the map.
